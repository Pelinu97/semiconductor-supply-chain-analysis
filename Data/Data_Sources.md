## Methodological Note

Figures cited in the analysis, including corporate financial data, semiconductor production indicators, and policy targets, are derived from dated public sources. Where estimates or contested figures are referenced, they are presented as indicative rather than definitive. Readers revisiting this project should consult updated source material for changes over time.
