# Visuals

This document describes planned visual products supporting the OSINT assessment. All visuals are based on publicly available sources and are intended for analytical illustration rather than operational intelligence use.

---

## 1. Global Semiconductor Supply Chain Map

### Purpose
Illustrate the geographic distribution and dependencies within the semiconductor ecosystem.

### Key Areas
- United States — chip design, EDA tools, and semiconductor innovation
- Taiwan — leading-edge manufacturing capacity (TSMC)
- South Korea — memory and advanced semiconductor production
- Netherlands — semiconductor manufacturing equipment (ASML)
- Japan — materials and industrial equipment
- China — major semiconductor market and expanding domestic capability

## 2. US–China Semiconductor Competition Overview

## 3. Turkey Semiconductor Exposure Assessment

### Purpose
Assess Turkey's dependency on imported semiconductor components across selected sectors.

### Sectors Covered
- Defense industry
- Automotive
- Telecommunications
- Industrial systems
- Consumer electronics

Assessment is based on publicly available industry reports and institutional sources.
